What to Expect on

Sunday

Whether you’ve followed Jesus for years or are simply exploring faith, we want your first Sunday to feel easy. Friendly volunteers can help you find the Worship Center, check in your kids, or answer questions. During the service, you can expect congregational worship, prayer, and biblical teaching from God’s Word. Come a few minutes early, and we’ll help you get settled.

Ministries for Every Stage of Life

Kids Ministry

Birth through 5th Grade

Bright, vibrant rooms with singing, activities, and engaging Bible lessons designed just for kids. Every Sunday, our team partners with parents to help kids know God’s Word and learn what it means to live and love like Jesus—all in a fun, safe environment designed for their age. First Baptist Learning Center, our weekday preschool, provides a Bible-based learning environment for young children throughout the week.

Ministries for Every Stage of Life

Student Ministry

Middle & High School

Missional Church One Students gives students in grades 6–12 a place to build friendships, ask honest questions, and grow in their relationship with Jesus. Each week includes worship, biblical teaching, and small groups led by caring adults who invest in students. Middle School meets Sundays at 9:15 AM, and High School meets at 10:45 AM, with additional opportunities throughout the week and year.

Special Needs Ministry

Every person is made in God’s image, and we want individuals with special needs and their families to have opportunities to grow, connect, and participate in the life of the church. Incredible Kids supports children with special needs, Incredible Fridays provides a monthly respite opportunity for families, and the Fellowship Life Group creates community for adults with special needs.

Serving & Missions

A Church That Shows Up
Beyond Our Walls

Missional Church One isn’t just a place you attend—it’s a people who show up. From opening our doors as an emergency warming shelter during extreme weather in partnership with Emmanuel Labor to serving alongside local organizations throughout Missional Church One , Christian outreach is one way we put our faith into action and care for our neighbors. We want to be a church our city can count on.

Get to Know Missional Church One

A Multigenerational Growing Church

Missional Church One is a multigenerational Christian community where kids, students, college students, young adults, families, and senior adults worship, grow, and serve together. We believe every generation has something to give and something to learn as we follow Jesus together.

Explore Our Ministries

Over 150 Years in Missional Church One

For more than 150 years, Missional Church One has faithfully served this community. The people, buildings, and neighborhoods have changed, but our commitment to helping people follow Jesus has remained. From the beginning, this church has looked beyond its walls to serve others and share the hope of Jesus. That same mission continues today as a Bible-teaching church serving families across Missional Church One , Texas, and Collin County.

Our Story

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to make disciples who live and love like Jesus and help others do the same. Everything we do flows from Jesus’ call to love God, love our neighbors, and make disciples. Led by Pastor Sam Holm, Missional Church One is committed to teaching the Bible faithfully and helping people put God’s Word into practice in everyday life (Matthew 22 and 28).
